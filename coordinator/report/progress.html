scratchy: the new way of working

Since the 2026-11-21 architecture discussion, scratchy stops fixing individual sites. The plan is to measure conformance per spec area with WPT, work through areas in dependency order, and hold speed to permanent budgets. This page is updated after every merged PR.

Step 2Engine becomes the default (QINC1l)merged #473
Step 3Conformance plan (QWPT1, R64)approved, all 8 answers
Step 4Run WPT JavaScript tests, report per areadone: baseline + CI gate (#504, #507)
Step 5Spec areas to conformance, in dependency orderlater

The order of the step 5 areas comes from the design memo. The current proposal is DOM core, events, the event loop and rendering steps, CSSOM, geometry, fetch and streams, then media.
